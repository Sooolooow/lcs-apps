Quick rules

1 amp per 0.001 in thickness is a solid starting point.
Back purge full-penetration joints on 316L to prevent sugaring — 10–20 cfh argon until cool.
Keep interpass under 300°F on multi-pass work. Faster travel + slightly higher amps = less total heat than a slow crawl.
Test on scrap before the part. Foot pedal: start 20–30%, ramp to full once the puddle forms.
